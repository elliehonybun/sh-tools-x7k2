4 · Method — the combined winter/summer formula the optimiser solves

Per element i (area A, orientation o, shading ratio P/H):
 • condi = Ai × Ui × κi  where κ = orientation-conduction factor × room × bedroom × level factors (winter conduction multipliers)
 • wgaini = Ai × SHGCi × Ew,i × ωi  where Ew = winter exposure (orientation, P/H, zone, slab/timber) and ω = winter radiation multipliers
 • sgaini = Ai × SHGCi × Es,i × σi  where Es = summer exposure (device ⇒ P/H = 2) and σ = summer radiation multipliers

Compliance conditions (constants Cu, Cshgc from zone, floor-type weighting, air movement):
 WINTER (CZ 2–8):  Σ condi / Σ wgaini ≤ Cu
 SUMMER (CZ 1–7):  Σ sgaini ≤ Cshgc × Areadwelling

Grouped by opening type g (caveat 1 — one U, one SHGC per group; Kg=Σκ·A, Wg=ΣEw·ω·A, Sg=ΣEs·σ·A):
 maximise  Σg Ag·Ug  (highest average U = most economical)
 subject to  Σg Kg·Ug − Cu·Σg Wg·SHGCg ≤ 0  (winter)
 and  Σg Sg·SHGCg ≤ Cshgc·Area  (summer)
 and  (Ug, SHGCg) ∈ WERS default library for that opening type  (caveat 2)

SHGC appears in both constraints with opposite pressure (winter wants it high, summer wants it low) which is why both are solved together: the search walks the discrete WERS catalogue per group (beam search over groups, feasibility-bounded), keeping the feasible combination with the highest area-weighted U.
